Before you buy

How do I get the book?

Right after checkout you get a download link by email. It's a PDF, so you can open it on your phone, tablet or computer straight away.

Do the fill-in boxes really work on a phone?

Yes. On iPhone, open it in Files or Books. On Android or a computer, open it in Adobe Acrobat Reader (free). Tap a box to tick it and tap a line to write. Save the file to keep your answers.

Will I actually see results?

The plan is built so that if you show up for the 36 workouts, your numbers go up. You test on day one and again in week 12, so you'll see exactly how far you came. What I can't do is the reps for you.

Do I need a gym?

No. You need a pull-up bar (a park, a gym or a doorway bar), something to dip on (parallel bars, two sturdy chairs or a bench) and a floor.

I'm a complete beginner. Is it for me?

Yes. The starting test puts you at the right level for each exercise, and every movement has an easier version. If you can't do a pull-up yet, you start with rows and negatives.

Do I have to box to use it?

No. The fighter lessons are about fear, ego, pressure and discipline, and they apply to school, work and training just as much as to the ring.
