# Two Versions of You — Production Blueprint

**Format:** TikTok · 1080×1440 (3:4) · 30 fps · H.264/AAC · ~56.7 s (52.7 s narration + 4 s end card)
**Audience:** young men 18–30 · **Arc:** recognition → guilt → desire to change → motivation → guide
**Main message:** Discipline is not a feeling, it's a decision you repeat — and the people around you won't make it easy.
**Narration:** `assets/audio/narration/two_men_narration.mp3` (ElevenLabs, Brian) — kept as recorded (short opening, no spoken CTA).

## Characters (one reusable rig, consistent across all scenes)
| Character | Look | Body language | Label |
|---|---|---|---|
| **THE FIRST ONE** | mid-gray skin, charcoal hoodie, crimson cap, dark joggers | slouched, head down, slow | `THE FIRST ONE` (crimson) |
| **THE SECOND ONE** | bright white, short dark hair, fitted tee / training top, white sneakers, subtle glow | upright, calm, deliberate | `THE SECOND ONE` (white) |
| **THE CIRCLE** (3 friends) | darker grays; ponytail, beanie, short hair; casual clothes | lounging, pointing, laughing | none |

## Visual rhythm
Detailed cinematic environments for story beats; hard cuts to **white figures on pure black** for concept beats (S1, S7, S11, S13).

## Subtitles
Narration captions in the top area, 2–5 words per chunk, word-by-word reveal synced to the real word timings (`two_men_words.json`). Cormorant Garamond italic for words, key word in crimson; Tenor Sans for labels. Speech bubbles are separate and only carry dialogue.

## Music
Original synthesized score (no licensed tracks): dark minimal pulse S1–S6 → drops out at S7 → tense low drone S8–S10 → rising, hopeful build S11–S12 → single held note S13 → soft resolve on end card. Narration ducks music by ~10 dB; final mix ≈ −14 LUFS for TikTok.

## Transitions
Hard cuts on narration beats; one whip-pan (S3→S4); black cuts before the "dark part" and before S11; fade into the end card.
